Leatherworking: easier to see in the dark

In a dark place the punched holes are dark dots with a thin tan ring, so you hear the punches before you see anything ("a thok before the animation"). Same timing and sounds in each; only how it looks changes. Now: as in game. L1 Light rings: each hole gets a thicker, lighter ring (the hole stays dark inside) and the thread is a shade brighter. L2 Punch flash: L1, plus a small warm flash right when each hole is punched, so every sound has something you see at that moment. L3 Glowing stitch: L2, and the thread draws through with a soft glow, and the final glint is a bit bigger. Sound is on.
