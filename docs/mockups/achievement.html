Achievement style, round 10

Three takes on Achievement, each one shape instead of a medallion next to a box. All three can be built from our own textures, the 3D item, and text. Pick a rarity, then Replay. Epic adds a glow to the rim; Legendary adds a second gold line.

Rarity

A. Capsule

A round emblem holds the item, which breaks out of its top. The emblem pops in, then opens into a bar to the right that carries the name. One rim runs around the whole shape, and a shine crosses it.

B. Plaque

A plaque with cut corners drops in, and the item rises out of it. The name is set into the plaque, with a gem on its top edge. Everything is centered.

C. Headline

No box at all. The item rises onto a small glowing disc, and the text sits beside it over a rule that draws in. A soft shadow behind the text keeps it readable.
