The info line, round 4: the item level without a badge, and a stronger Aura

Relic and Beam: Gem (the number on a small faceted gem in the rarity color, at the end of the line) or Readout (no frame: the number in the rarity color over a tiny ITEM LEVEL, to the right of the name). Upgrade adds the change against what you have equipped (green up, gray down). Aura: the game today next to the new Aura (a heartbeat on arrival then a slow breath, a narrow sunbeam with dust drifting through it, the rarity word between fading lines, a bigger name), with the gem or the readout. Splash: the block slanted with the banner (the game's is upright) or the gem. Replay restarts the animations.
