The info line in the other styles

Achievement shows the item level in a small round badge, so the line under the name keeps only the type. The same for Relic, Beam, Aura, Banner and Splash: Before is what's in the game ("Two-Handed Axes · item level 63"), After moves the item level into the same badge, placed where each style has room, and puts the rarity word in its color at the front of the line (Aura and Splash already say the rarity elsewhere, so they keep just the type). Items without an item level (bags, potions) look as before.
