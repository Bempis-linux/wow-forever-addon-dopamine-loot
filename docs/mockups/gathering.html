Sound

1. The bag under pressure

With 4 slots or fewer, each item pumps the bag up a notch: it swells, holds, and only partly deflates, so it stays bigger each time (1). It strains: the drawstring pulls tight, the stitches stretch with light glowing through, and at the last slot it trembles at rest (2). Full: the next item makes it strain, the drawstring snaps open, a puff of loot spills out the top and gets sucked back in, and it shakes "nope" (3). Release: free up space and it lets out a "pfff", shrinking back with a puff of air (4). The count bubble (6) is an option.

2. Gathering

Every gather: a tiny burst in the node's style (8); the pieces fly into the harvest tally (10); each gather in a run plays a soft note a step higher (11; the ♪ shows the step), resetting after a pause. Now and then: a material reaching a full stack flashes its row with a stack mark (12); a skill-up shows your new skill with a bar to the next unlock (14). Rare: a gem, rare herb or rare fish gets a bigger burst and the normal popup (16). Stop: after a while without gathering the tally folds into a one-line recap, then fades (17). Calm: after many gathers in a row, bursts shrink and go silent; the tally and rare finds stay full (18).

3. Crafting

Create All (21): instead of a popup per item, each result hops from the station onto one pile that counts up, and the batch ends with a closing flourish and one line, "×12 Silk Bandage". Skill-up (22): the same bar as gathering. Recipe color (23): when your skill turns a recipe from orange to yellow (or yellow to green, green to gray), a small heads-up says so, since that's when skill-ups slow down.
