Blacksmithing, round 1

Four stagings, each its own design (your picks): on the cast bar, beside it, at the item in the profession window, around your character. A craft is a 3 s cast with three hammer beats and the payoff when the item is made. Simple shapes, the real item icon, sounds that feel like metal. Space replays; keys 1-4.
