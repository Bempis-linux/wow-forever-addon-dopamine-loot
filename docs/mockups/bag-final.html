The bulging bag: final version

Built from what's in game (the backpack icon with the game's quality border, breathing amber to red, the slots left in the corner). Every idea you picked is a switch: turn one off to compare. Swelling (4 slots left to 1): the item drops in and the bag gulps, it sags heavier, lumps press out, it creaks at 2 and 1 left with a faster heartbeat. Full: the item bounces off back to the loot window, a padlock clunks shut, and trying again soon gets a bigger reaction (a harder shake, then a tired slump). Release: a relief settle with a gold glint around the border, a green "+N", a dust puff. Sound is off; turn it on for the gulp, creak, clunk and sigh sketches.
