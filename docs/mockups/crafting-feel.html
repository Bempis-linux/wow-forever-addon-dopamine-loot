Crafting feel, round 3

Built on what made Leatherworking and Alchemy your favorites: a short rhythm of physical work beats with tactile sounds, then one clear payoff, all in about a second. Every single craft now has that shape, with a little randomness so the 100th doesn't feel like a copy. Batch touch is what each item of a Create All gets: only the payoff, tiny, its pitch walking up the batch (like the gathering notes). "×30 fast" is a long batch, to hear whether it still feels good after many. Sound matters here: turn it on.

Skill-up bars: the material is the bar

Tailoring's stitched bar works because the pattern is the bar, not a decoration on it. Here every profession gets the same treatment: the bar is made of its craft's material, and filling it does what that material does. "Skill up" fills each one a step; "All" plays them together.
