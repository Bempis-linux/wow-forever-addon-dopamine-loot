## Summary (one sentence, shown in search results)

(Live on CurseForge, as written there by the user:) A fun addon that uses visuals and sounds to spice up
simple looting and quest rewards.

(First draft:) Turns every drop into a small celebration: items pop up turning in 3D, and big hauls burst from the
ground as a rain of loot and coins.

## Description

Paste with the editor in Markdown mode. The "What's new" block at the top is replaced on each minor release (1.x.0) with that release's highlights; patch releases (1.x.y) leave it.

```markdown
# ✨ Loot that feels like loot ✨

Every drop gets its moment. Items pop up beside you **turning in 3D**, big hauls **burst out of the ground** as a rain of loot and coins, and your professions get their own little shows.

---

## 🆕 What's new in 1.3.0: the professions update

- ⛏️ **Gathering:** every herb, ore, fish and skin bursts where you gathered it, with its own sound, into a new **harvest window**.
- 🔨 **Crafting:** a small touch of your profession plays by your cast bar, and **Create All** piles everything up under the profession window.
- 📈 **Skill-ups:** a bar made of your profession's material, with a little tool that moves on each point.
- 🎒 **Bulging bag:** your backpack swells as your bags fill and locks up when they're full.
- 📋 **Side column:** small loot lists quietly as rows instead of popping up.
- ✋ **Move frames:** drag the popups, the side column and the skill-up banner wherever you like.

Full notes in the changelog.

---

## 🎁 Loot popups

- 🗡️ **The real item, in 3D:** weapons, shields, helms and shoulders turn beside your character; everything else shows as a tilting card.
- 🎨 **Eight styles:** Relic, Beam, Banner, Splash, Aura, Achievement, Rune decode and Forged letters, each with a live preview.
- 💎 **Arrivals by rarity**, your pick for each: fireflies or an emerald flare for green, a sapphire flare or an aurora for blue, an implosion, eclipse or starfall for purple. A **Legendary** dims the screen and a comet streaks down before a supernova.
- 🤫 **Out of your way:** clicks pass straight through, hover a card to keep it, and in a fight it stays small.

## 🌧️ Loot rain

- 💥 **Big hauls explode:** loot three or more things, a pile of gold, or a quest reward, and it all flies up from the ground: as a **Fountain**, a **Quick burst** or a **Big bang**.
- 🏆 **Best for last:** the best item waits a beat, flies highest and slams down, harder the rarer it is.
- 🪙 **Coins that shine:** silver merges into gold, and a tally counts your money up above the bag.
- 🔦 **Beams and names** over Rare and better; gray junk lands dull and heavy.
- 🎒 **Clean finish:** everything gathers into your bag, one by one or in one quick vacuum.
- ⚔️ Waits until combat is over. **Escape** skips it.

## ⛏️ Gathering

- 🌿 **A burst for each profession:** a leaf pop for herbs, rock strikes for ore, a splash for fish, a knife glint for skins.
- ✨ **Rare finds** get their own moment: a golden seed that bursts open, a gem flare, a leaping fish, a double slash or a flurry.
- 📊 **Harvest window:** your session by profession, how fast you're gathering, what it's worth, and a gold badge when a stack fills up. It fades when you stop and hides in combat.
- 🎵 Soft notes climb with each gather, and it all **calms down by itself** on long farming runs.

## 🔨 Crafting and skill-ups

- 🧵 **Every craft gets a touch of its profession** by your cast bar: punches in a leather strip, a potion boiling over, stitches and a snip, gears, a sizzle and a flip, hammer taps, runes, a green plus.
- 📦 **Create All** drops each item into a growing pile in a tray under the profession window, with a count, and a shake and sparkle at the end. Long batches stay calm: the sounds step down after the first item.
- 📈 **Skill-up banner:** your new skill and a bar toward what's next, made of your profession's material (stitched cloth, punched leather, a glass tube, glowing runes, stone, water...) with a little tool that moves on each point. Prefer it plain? Pick the **simple bar**.
- 🌱 Reach a new herb or ore and it **shows you what you can gather now**; after a crafting skill-up it tells you when a recipe changed color.

## 🎒 Bags and the side column

- 🎒 **Bulging bag:** nearly full, your backpack swells with each item, sags and creaks, and shows the slots left. Full, items bounce off and a padlock snaps shut.
- 📋 **Side column:** small finds (and everything during a fight) list as rows under "Picked up" and leave after a few seconds.

## 🔊 Sounds

- A sound per rarity, picked **by ear** from the game's own sounds, plus our own chimes, coins and thuds that **climb in pitch** as the loot keeps coming.

## ⚙️ Settings

Type **`/dl`** (or Options › AddOns › Dopamine Loot). Every feature has its own switch on the first page, and every page has **Test buttons**, so you can see it all without looting a thing.

| Page | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Dopamine Loot** | every feature on or off, move frames, quick tests |
| **Popups** | style, size, placement, the 3D item, with a live preview |
| **Arrivals** | how each rarity arrives |
| **Loot rain** | when it rains, the animation, where it flies from, every effect |
| **Sounds** | the sound for each rarity |
| **Professions** | gathering, the Create All pile, the skill-up bar look |

---

- 🎮 Made for **WoW: Forever**
- 🔒 Saves only your settings. Sends nothing anywhere, prints nothing to chat.
- 🐞 Bugs and ideas: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Bempis-linux/wow-forever-addon-dopamine-loot/issues)
```
