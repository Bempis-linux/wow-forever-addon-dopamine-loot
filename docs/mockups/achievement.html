Achievement style, round 12

Capsule (your pick), Crest and Framed toast, plus three new takes in the same direction. Each is one shape. All can be built from our own textures, the 3D item, and text. Pick a rarity, then Replay. Epic adds a glow to the rim; Legendary adds a second gold line.

Rarity

A. Capsule your pick

A round emblem holds the item, which breaks out of its top. The emblem pops in, then opens into a bar to the right that carries the name. One rim runs around the whole shape, and a shine crosses it.

D. Crest new

A hexagon crest holds the item. A bar with a pointed end runs right, and rarity pips sit under the name (1 for Uncommon, up to 4 for Legendary). A small shield at the end pops in with the item level, like achievement points.

E. Framed toast

A finished game-style frame. The item stands in a recessed well on the left and breaks out of its top, and a rarity tab sits on the top edge. A light runs once around the frame's edge as it lands.

F. Swallowtail new

Capsule's emblem, but the bar ends in a forked ribbon tail that fades into the rarity color. A thin inner line runs inside both the emblem and the bar, like an engraved trim.

G. Diamond HUD new

Sleek and modern. The item stands in a diamond. To its right a bar of dark glass fades out, with a bright edge only on its left, corner brackets, and the name in capitals over a line that draws in.

H. Arch new

A tall arched window holds the item upright, so long weapons and staves fit. A keystone gem crowns the arch, and the name bar is tucked against it on the right.
