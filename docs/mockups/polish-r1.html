Final polish: skill-ups and the Create All heap

Built from your in-game screenshots. Each banner shows Now (as the game draws it today) beside Polished, on the screenshots' bright ground or a dark one. Every change is a switch, so you can keep only the ones you like. "Skill up" fills the bar and plays the tool's motion; there's no sound on this page. Nothing here is in the game yet.

1. Skill-up banners

  • Centered bar: with no next-herb icon at the end (every crafting profession, Fishing, Skinning), the bar sits under the title instead of 12 px to the left.
  • Room for the note: a little gap under the bar and a dark outline on "13 to 150", so it doesn't touch the bar and reads on bright ground (on the Fishing one it was half hidden).
  • Soft shade: a faint dark halo behind the banner, so the white title and the bar read on sunny ground (no box, no border).
  • Bigger tools: the tools are about a third bigger; on the screenshots the pan, the paperclip, the pick and the knife were hard to make out.
  • Stronger glow: the title's glow in the profession's color shows more (it barely showed).
  • Materials: Mining's stone a bit lighter with bigger copper flecks, Skinning's fur grain stronger (it read as a plain tan bar), a shine line on Alchemy's and Fishing's liquid, Cooking's grill marks finer.

2. The Create All heap

Now: each item drops to a spot picked in advance, so it can stop in mid-air beside the others ("it slightly falls and just randomly sticks"). The new ones all land on what's already there. H1 Pile up: each item falls into the middle and slides down the slope until it rests on the floor or on other items, so the heap grows into a mound. H2 Neat rows: items stack in rows from the floor up, each row a little shorter, like goods stacked on a counter. H3 Tumble: like H1, but each item hits the heap with a little bounce and rolls a step down the slope before it settles, turning as it goes.
