Achievement style, round 16

Capsule 3 is the keeper for Achievement; Brush stroke is kept as a unique style. Below: five fantasy uniques, then the two round 15 ideas you liked. Pick a rarity, then Replay.

Rarity

Keepers

Capsule 3, badge and gauge

The Achievement style: the plain Capsule with the item-level badge, the rarity gauge, a thin bevel and the emblem's inner ring.

U1. Brush stroke

One thick, rough stroke of paint in the item's color swipes across, the name over it in big white italic, flecks flying off the end.

Fantasy uniques

Frameless, the letters themselves are the show. F1 is Glitch done as magic; F2 and F3 are Kinetic type done as a scribe and a forge. The name font here is a stand-in for the game's Morpheus or Friz Quadrata.

F1. Rune decode

Every letter starts as a glowing rune that flickers through other runes, then settles into its letter, left to right.

F2. Quill script

A glowing nib writes the name in italic, left to right, the fresh ink glowing as it dries. A swash underline is drawn last.

F3. Forged letters

Each letter is struck in white-hot and cools through orange to its color; sparks jump off every strike.

F4. Mist summon

The item and the name gather out of a coloured mist: wide and blurred, they pull together sharp while the mist drifts off.

F5. Ember burn-in

A glowing ember edge sweeps across and the name burns into view behind it, embers floating up off the edge.

Round 15 ideas you liked

U6. Kinetic type

Only letters. The rarity word drifts by huge and hollow behind; the name's letters drop in one by one with a bounce, and a thin line grows under them.

U7. Glitch

The name arrives split into red and cyan copies, jittering and sliced, then snaps together clean. The rarity types itself in under it with a blinking cursor.
