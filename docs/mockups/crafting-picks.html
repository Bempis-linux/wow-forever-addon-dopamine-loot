Gathering skill bars: new, to match (proposals)

Now is the plain bar the game shows today. New gives each gathering profession a material and a tool, the same way as the crafting bars, keeping the icon of the next herb or ore at the end. Herbalism: a green stem with small leaves; a sprout pops up and puffs pollen. Mining: layered stone with copper flecks; a pick taps and knocks off chips. Fishing: water with a wobbling surface; a bobber dips and sends a ripple. Skinning: a hide with a fur grain; a knife glints across. Simple shapes only; the tools are small textures in game.
