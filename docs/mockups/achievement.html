Achievement style, round 17

Capsule 3 is the keeper for Achievement; Brush stroke is kept as a unique style. Below: the four fantasy uniques, polished, then the two round 15 ideas you liked. Pick a rarity, then Replay.

Rarity

Keepers

Capsule 3, badge and gauge

The Achievement style: the plain Capsule with the item-level badge, the rarity gauge, a thin bevel and the emblem's inner ring.

U1. Brush stroke

One thick, rough stroke of paint in the item's color swipes across, the name over it in big white italic, flecks flying off the end.

Fantasy uniques

Frameless; the letters are the show. All four share one layout: the name, a small diamond divider, one info line. Sparks, mist and embers grow with rarity: try Legendary. The serif stands in for the game's Morpheus or Friz Quadrata.

F1. Rune decode

Each letter holds its place as a glowing rune flickering through other runes. Left to right they settle into letters with a white flash, and each rune floats off as it goes.

F2. Forged letters

Each letter is struck in white-hot with a flash behind it, then cools through orange to its color. Sparks arc off every strike and fall; the divider cools last.

F3. Mist summon

Puffs of colored mist swirl in from all sides and meet, then breathe out in a soft ring as the item and the name gather sharp. A low mist stays under the name.

F4. Ember burn-in

An ember edge sweeps across; the letters burn in glowing hot just behind it, then cool to their color. Embers and a little smoke rise off the edge.

Round 15 ideas you liked

U6. Kinetic type

Only letters. The rarity word drifts by huge and hollow behind; the name's letters drop in one by one with a bounce, and a thin line grows under them.

U7. Glitch

The name arrives split into red and cyan copies, jittering and sliced, then snaps together clean. The rarity types itself in under it with a blinking cursor.
