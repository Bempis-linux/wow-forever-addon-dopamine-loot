Achievement style, round 14

Back to the plain Capsule, each with one or two additions only. The rim is the only thing that changes with rarity: its color, a glow from Epic, a gold rim for Legendary. No orbits, filigree or loops beyond the item's sway. Pick a rarity, then Replay.

Rarity

1. Capsule, item level badge

The item level moves into a small round badge that closes the bar's end and pops on last.

2. Capsule, rarity gauge

Four small bars beside the rarity, filled up to it. The item level stays in the line under the name.

3. Capsule, badge and gauge

Both, plus a thin bevel on the rim and an inner ring on the emblem: the most this direction should carry.

Unique styles, in Splash's spirit

No frame at all: one bold shape and its motion carry the moment, like Splash's slashing banner.

U1. Brush stroke

One thick, rough stroke of paint in the item's color swipes across, and the name is written over it in big white italic. Flecks of paint fly off the stroke's end.

U2. Neon sign

The name is a neon tube that flickers on (two stutters, then steady), with a neon outline arching over the item. A soft glow spreads on the wall behind it.

U3. Torn reveal

A strip of the screen rips open with jagged edges, showing the rarity's color inside, and the item and name sit in the tear.
