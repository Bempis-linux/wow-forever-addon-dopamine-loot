The bulging bag, and gathering that pops

1. The bulging bag

Our own drawing over the game's backpack button (the game's frames stay untouched). The bag is drawn a little fuller the fuller your bags are. With a few slots left, each item that goes in makes it swell and wobble, the stitches glow amber and a small bubble says how many are left. When it's full, the item bounces off the bag, the bag shakes "no" with a puff of dust, and the bubble turns red: Bags full. Press Loot an item a few times at each fullness.

2. Gathering pops

Made to stay fun for hours: small, quick and quiet. Node pop: a tiny burst in the node's own style where you gathered (leaves and petals, rock chips with a spark, a splash ring, tufts) and the pieces arc toward the tally. Harvest tally: a small list at the side counting this session's materials, the new ones popping "+2"; it fades out after a while without gathering. Rare find (a gem from a vein, a rare herb, a rare fish): a bigger burst and the normal popup. Skill up: your new skill with a bar to the next unlock. Calm: after many gathers in a row the pops get smaller and silent, the tally keeps counting.
