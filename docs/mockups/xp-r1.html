XP, round 1

No new bars: everything happens on the game's own XP bar. The game fills its bar the moment you get XP, so we cover the new part and reveal it as the orbs land. The orbs come from your character (an addon can't know where the mob was on screen). Milestones: the +10% tick and the level-up screen in the Ring + road look. Space replays; keys 1-4 switch the orbs.
