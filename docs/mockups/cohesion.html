Popups and the harvest window: one layout

Your pick: two lanes + one row look. Big cards (your popup style, a stand-in here) stay right of your character for the moments that matter. Everything small (the compact popups) leaves that stack and becomes a row in the right-hand column, in the same look as the harvest window: icon with quality border, name in its color, gold count; the same item twice merges into "×2". A loot with a big item and small ones: the big one gets its card, the small ones go to the column (or tuck under the card: "With it" below). Three ways to arrange the column; "Show zones" outlines the screen's areas. Note: with a column on the right edge, the big cards' default spot moves a little toward your character (55% across instead of 63%) so they never overlap it: at 63% a 300-wide card ends at 1160 and the column starts at 1118.
