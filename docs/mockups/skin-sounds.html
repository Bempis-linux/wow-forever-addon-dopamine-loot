Skinning sounds

Our own synthesized candidates, all at the same loudness. "Now" is what's in game. The normal sound plays on every skin, so it should stay pleasant after hundreds; the rare ones can be bigger. Click ▶ to play; "×5" plays a normal one five times in a row, the way a skinning run sounds.
