1. The bag under pressure

B1 Two looks: Game art (in game: the backpack button's own icon, puffing up inside a ring of ours that glows and splits under strain) or Leather bag (round 2's, drawn by us). B2 Each item lands with a squash (wide and short), a stretch, then a short wobble as it settles; switch to "Straight" to compare with round 2. B4 Full: the drawstring whips out as a line with a tiny "pop" ring. B5 Freeing 1 slot is a small sigh; freeing 6 is a big exhale. B6 An herb going into an herb bag gets a small green glint and doesn't strain the bag. B7 The full moment plays on the game's real "Inventory is full" error, at most once every 4 s; looting again sooner gets a small "nope" only (try clicking Loot fast at 0). B9 The switch and Test bag button (top left) are how it would look on the settings page.
