Dopamine Loot: render the mockups' sounds and our tool art

Served by tools/render-media.ps1. renderAll() renders every sound offline (silent: an OfflineAudioContext, nothing plays) and every picture to a 64x64 PNG, and posts each to the receiver, which saves it into tools/out/. tools/convert-media.sh turns them into the addon's OGG and TGA files. The sound code is copied from docs/mockups/crafting-picks.html; the pictures are our own drawings.
