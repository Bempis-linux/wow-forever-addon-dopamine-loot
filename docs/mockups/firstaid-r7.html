First Aid, round 7: the plus

Every First Aid version so far that ends in the green plus, plus three new ones built only around the plus (simple shapes: two bars, a glow, a ring). It plays where the game now puts craft effects: right of the cast bar. Now in game is round 4 (the roll, three wraps, a small plus rising). Round 1 Heartbeat: a ribbon unrolls, wraps once, the plus beats twice. R5 F2 Stitch a cross: four strokes stitch a plus, then it glows. R5 F4 Pulse: two heartbeats light the plus from dim to bright, a wrap, a green ring. New: P1 Plus pop: just the plus popping in with a soft glow and one ring. P2 Plus draws: the upright bar grows, then the crossbar (a soft tap each), a flash and glints at its four tips. P3 Plus heartbeat: the plus beats twice, brighter each time, then one ring. Sound is on.
