Skill-up, round 2

Your three picks from round 1, in your order, now made for skill-ups. Each profession keeps its own identity: the bar is drawn in its material, its tool (the same drawings as in game) rides the tip and strikes on every skill-up, and the herbs and ores you'll unlock are part of the design. XP and rep come later. Space replays; keys 1-3 switch the family, Q-T the profession.
