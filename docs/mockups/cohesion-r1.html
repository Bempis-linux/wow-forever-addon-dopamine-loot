What sticks out (my read)

  1. Panels: three styles. The Create All tray (tooltip box on brown) is the odd one out; the 1.4 pieces use dark rounded panels with a gold hairline; lists use fading rows.
  2. Golds: five in the code, a sixth in the mockups. Two would do: our accent gold and the game's.
  3. Titles: the 1.4 designs put a serif on many titles; in game that's Morpheus. Proposal: Morpheus only for item names and the biggest titles, Friz Quadrata for the rest.
  4. The Ring family already ties 1.4 together; the 1.3 skill-up bar it replaces was the last bar-shaped banner. The harvest window and the bag have no ring, which is fine (they aren't progress).
  5. Counters: each system colors its numbers (gold money, purple XP, green rep, profession color). Keep.
