Leatherworking, round 2: easier to see in the dark

Round 1's versions were too alike. These three are clearly different ways to make the four punches, the stitch and the tug read on dark ground (same timing and sounds in each). Now: as in game. A Bold and lit: everything bigger; the holes are cream rings that glow, a ring bursts out of each punch, the thread is thick and glowing. B Leather strip: a plain tan band (the leather) slides in under the work first, so the dark holes and the cream stitch show against it on any ground; a little dust on each punch. C Light dots and sparks: the opposite of now: each punch leaves a glowing warm dot and throws a few sparks, and the thread lights up from dot to dot. Sound is on.
