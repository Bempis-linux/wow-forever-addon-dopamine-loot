Achievement style, round 13

Capsule is the base: the round emblem opening into a bar, with Crest's item-level shield and the rarity gauge. Three emblem shapes (coin, gem cut, seal). Rims are metal by rarity (iron, steel, silver, gold), and each rarity adds a piece. Everything comes in order: the emblem rolls in, the bar unspools, the gauge fills, the shield clicks on. Pick a rarity, then Replay.

Rarity

1. Coin new

A struck coin with a milled edge. It rolls in like a coin on its edge, and the rim light turns slowly while it's up.

2. Gem cut new

An octagon cut like a gem: facets catch the light from the top left, and the item sits in the table facet.

3. Seal new

A round seal with a scalloped edge and an embossed ring of dots, like a stamp of approval.

What each rarity adds. Uncommon: an iron rim, 1 gauge segment. Rare: steel, a fine inner trim on the emblem, 2 segments. Epic: silver, gems orbiting the emblem, 3 segments. Legendary: gold, filigree on the emblem, a breathing halo behind it, 4 segments.
Space. The tag line carries the rarity and a NEW mark the first time you loot the item. The line under the name has the type and, for an upgrade, how many item levels it beats what you wear. The item level itself moves to the shield.
In game. The frames, rim and shield are our own generated textures, tinted by rarity. The gauge segments are one small texture. The living details are an OnUpdate on the shown popup only; they stop when it hides.
